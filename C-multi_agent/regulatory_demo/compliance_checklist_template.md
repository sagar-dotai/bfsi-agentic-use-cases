Compliance Checklist Template

- Identify applicable regulator and notice ID
- Extract explicit obligations (encryption, retention, rotation, disclosure timelines)
- Map obligations to affected systems and owners
- For each obligation: define evidence artifacts required (configs, screenshots, logs)
- Draft remediation tasks with acceptance criteria
- Assign owners and deadlines aligned to regulator deadline
- Generate audit-ready report and evidence bundle
- Schedule attestation and internal review
